Data sources

OpenAlex

  • OpenAlex source and work metadata are dedicated to the public domain under CC0.
  • Used for optional enrichment or cross-checking by the Emerging Journals Database 2026, Abstract-to-Journal Matcher, OA & APC Explorer, Journal Trust Profile, Identifier & Citation Toolkit, and Reference Integrity Checker.
  • Requests identify the site contact address (Contact (at) smbajwa.com) through the documented mailto parameter. Responses are cached in the browser for 30 days and can be cleared by the user; there is no repository snapshot.

Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)

  • DOAJ journal and article metadata are CC0.
  • Used on demand for journal, open-access, licence, waiver, and fee declarations by the Abstract-to-Journal Matcher, OA & APC Explorer, Journal Trust Profile, and Identifier & Citation Toolkit. Responses are cached locally for 30 days and can be cleared.

Crossref

  • Crossref REST API bibliographic facts are retrieved live after user confirmation by the Abstract-to-Journal Matcher, Journal Trust Profile, Identifier & Citation Toolkit, and Reference Integrity Checker.
  • Abstracts are not requested for the matcher and are otherwise discarded rather than stored or exported. Responses are cached in the browser for 30 days and can be cleared; there is no repository snapshot.

Retraction Watch via Crossref

  • The Retraction Watch database integrated into Crossref is CC0 and is used by the Reference Integrity Checker to classify Crossref update-to metadata.
  • Records are retrieved live after user confirmation and cached in browser-local IndexedDB for 30 days; no snapshot is included. Retraction Watch is named as requested, with no affiliation or endorsement implied.

ORCID and SCImago

  • ORCID is used only for local ISO 7064 checksum validation and links to public records. No ORCID API credentials, client secrets, source code, or public-data files are included. ORCID is a trademark of ORCID, Inc.; this site is not affiliated with or endorsed by ORCID.
  • SCImago is linked only as an external ISSN lookup. No SCImago or Scopus-derived values are bundled or distributed.

Local and linked journal material

  • The Emerging Journals Database 2026 uses the translated title, ISSN, discipline, and supplied quartile list in its local data file. Journal cards can request live OpenAlex enrichment and link to SCImago.
  • The Abstract-to-Journal Matcher uses compact original aggregate topic labels joined to smbajwa.com’s curated journal identities; it contains no copied abstracts or publisher scope prose.
  • The Journal Trust Profile local search seed was derived on 2026-10-07 from the Emerging Journals data and is explicitly labelled as an smbajwa.com source.
  • The OA & APC Explorer local journal snapshot is intentionally empty; its data manifest records source and exclusion boundaries. OpenAPC is linked only as an independent historical-cost resource; no OpenAPC ODbL/DbCL data are included, queried, cached, or merged.

Scope and limits by tool

Emerging Journals Database 2026

The citation score is an OpenAlex two-year measure, not a Journal Impact Factor. Supplied quartiles are presented as supplied and are not endorsements or quality verdicts. No Clarivate, Scopus, CAS, Journal Impact Factor, blacklist, watchlist, or cover-image data are included; the warning-list field remains disabled because redistribution terms are unclear. Listings and metrics can be incomplete.

Abstract-to-Journal Matcher

Lexical similarity is a discovery lead, not an acceptance prediction, ranking, endorsement, quality score, or substitute for reading a journal’s current aims and scope. No Clarivate, Scopus, CAS, or Journal Impact Factor values are used.

Journal Trust Profile

The profile is not an endorsement, blacklist, legal finding, or substitute for current journal policies. Missing metadata is not evidence of misconduct. It includes no Clarivate, Scopus, CAS, JIF, blacklist, or watchlist data.

OA & APC Explorer

Fees and waivers are publisher declarations in DOAJ, not quotes or live currency conversion. OpenAlex counts are context, not quality measures. No OpenAPC, Clarivate, Scopus, CAS, or Journal Impact Factor data are integrated, and no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Identifier & Citation Toolkit

Metadata can be incomplete or conflicting, and no identifier database proves a record complete or correct. Follow linked primary sources. ORCID use and trademark status are described above.

Reference Integrity Checker

No database establishes that a paper is error-free. “No alert found” means only that no matching update was returned by the queried source; it does not infer misconduct. Evidence can be missing, delayed, or conflicting.

Plant Lab Calculator Suite

For research planning only. Verify formula assumptions and laboratory protocols. The Wallace method is a rough short-oligo screen; primer melting-temperature output is not primer design or specificity analysis.
