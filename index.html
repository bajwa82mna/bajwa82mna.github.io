Research

I study how horticultural crops respond to abiotic stress, with a focus on tomato and fruit quality.

Abiotic stress

Plant responses to heat, drought and salinity, a question of crop resilience under a changing climate.

Fruit quality

Tomato and other fruit crops, including nutritional quality and the vitamin C question.

Gene regulation

Alternative splicing and how gene expression is reshaped when plants are stressed.

Calcium signalling

Calmodulin-like (CML) proteins as candidate decoders of stress signals.
