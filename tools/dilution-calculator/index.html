About and limitations

The calculator converts compatible molar units or compatible mass/volume units. It does not convert between those families because molecular weight would be required. Percent means % w/v. It flags C2 greater than C1 as impossible by dilution alone. Confirm pipette limits and laboratory protocols independently. See methods.

Frequently asked questions

What equation is used?

C1V1 equals C2V2.

Can it convert molar and mass units?

No; molecular weight is required.

What does percent mean?

Percent is treated as weight per volume.

Can it concentrate a solution?

No; dilution cannot make C2 exceed C1.

Are values uploaded?

No; calculations run in the browser.
