# Methods

The calculator rearranges `C1 × V1 = C2 × V2`. Molar units convert to mol/L; mass concentration units convert to mg/mL (`1% w/v = 10 mg/mL`). Molar and mass units are not interconverted because that requires molecular weight. Serial dilutions use `transfer = final volume / factor` and `diluent = final volume − transfer`.
