About and limitations

Whitespace is removed and case is normalized. Standard IUPAC symbols including N are supported. GC% counts literal G and C; ambiguous bases remain in the denominator. This tool does not validate biological direction, reading frames, or sequence identity. See methods.

Frequently asked questions

Are IUPAC codes supported?

Yes, standard ambiguous DNA and RNA codes are supported.

Can I paste multiple records?

Yes, use FASTA headers for multiple records.

How is GC percent calculated?

Literal G and C bases are divided by total sequence length.

What happens to invalid characters?

The tool stops and names the invalid characters.

Is the sequence uploaded?

No; transformation stays in the browser.
