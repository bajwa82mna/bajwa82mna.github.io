# Methods

Sequences are uppercased after FASTA headers and whitespace are removed. Complements follow standard IUPAC nucleotide pairings: A↔T/U, C↔G, R↔Y, K↔M, B↔V, D↔H, while S, W, and N are self-complementary. GC% counts literal G and C bases divided by total sequence length; ambiguous codes are retained in the denominator.
