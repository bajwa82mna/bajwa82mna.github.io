How to use Journal Hub

  1. Search by title or ISSN, or paste an abstract in the private matcher.
  2. Select a result to open its combined journal profile and evidence links.
  3. Review the selected journal across the available evidence sources, use the OA/APC tray to compare up to four journals if needed, then verify important details at the source.

Worked example

Search for Plant Journal, open its combined profile, then compare the source dates, OA declarations and timing coverage before following the journal’s own submission guidance. The profile is evidence to inspect, not a recommendation.

About this evidence

Journal Hub joins the Emerging Journals 2026 snapshot with open OpenAlex enrichment and partial DOAJ/Crossref timing evidence. Crossref medians describe published articles with deposited dates; they exclude rejected manuscripts and records without usable dates. DOAJ timing is journal-reported. This evidence is not a service guarantee.

Acceptance rate: Not openly available. It is never inferred from published articles. No JIF, Clarivate, Scopus, CAS or bundled SJR data is included. The abstract matcher is private and lexical; topical overlap does not establish suitability, quality or acceptance likelihood.

Frequently asked questions

Is abstract matching private?

Yes. Matching is local and no abstract text is sent to an API.

What does a match score mean?

Only relative lexical overlap with compact journal profiles; it is not a quality or acceptance score.

Why is acceptance rate unavailable?

No suitable open source provides a dependable denominator, so it is shown as Not openly available.

How should timing evidence be read?

As partial historical evidence with coverage counts, not a promise about your manuscript.

Are APCs current?

Publisher declarations can change. Verify fees, taxes and waivers on the linked source before deciding.

Does a missing trust signal mean a journal is unsafe?

No. Missing means the open sources checked did not provide that evidence.

Does the hub reproduce commercial rankings?

No. It contains no Clarivate, Scopus, CAS, JIF or bundled SJR data.
