Sources, scope and privacy

Journal and fee declarations: DOAJ metadata (CC0). Scholarly context: OpenAlex source metadata (CC0). OpenAlex counts are contextual and not quality measures. This tool does not include OpenAPC, Clarivate, Scopus, CAS or Journal Impact Factor data. Search requests occur only after you submit; filters, comparisons, budgets and exports stay in your browser.
