Title: Drought-responsive root traits and gene expression improve tomato resilience

Abstract: We measured root architecture, stomatal conductance, photosynthetic performance, and antioxidant activity in cultivated tomato exposed to progressive water deficit. RNA sequencing identified abscisic-acid and aquaporin pathways associated with deeper roots and recovery after rewatering. The results connect physiological responses with candidate genes for drought-resilient tomato breeding.

Keywords: tomato, drought, root architecture, transcriptomics, abscisic acid, crop improvement
