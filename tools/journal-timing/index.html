About this tool

This snapshot joins the 22,281-journal Emerging Journals 2026 dataset to DOAJ and Crossref by print ISSN or e-ISSN only. See methods and Credits and data sources.

Why acceptance rate is unavailable

There is no suitable open source for this field. It is shown as “Not openly available” and is never estimated from published articles, because published articles omit rejected manuscripts.

Limitations

Coverage is partial; publisher deposits and self-reports may be absent, stale, or defined differently. Crossref medians exclude rejected manuscripts and any published records without usable dates. Values are descriptive evidence, not service guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

What does DOAJ weeks mean?

It is the journal's self-reported average number of weeks from submission to publication in DOAJ.

What do the Crossref medians measure?

They summarize dates deposited for accepted, published articles in the sampled three-year window.

Why are some medians blank?

A median is suppressed unless at least 10 valid observations exist for that interval.

Does missing mean fast?

No. Missing means the relevant open timing evidence was unavailable in this snapshot.

Are these acceptance rates?

No. Published articles provide no rejected-manuscript denominator, so acceptance rates are not estimated.

Can journals be compared directly?

Use caution because deposits and editorial workflows differ and coverage is incomplete.
