About and limitations

SD uses n−1, quartiles use type-7 interpolation, and outliers use the 1.5×IQR rule. Student-t critical values are computed for the exact sample degrees of freedom; very small groups produce wide or undefined intervals. Flags do not justify deleting observations, and descriptive summaries do not test hypotheses. See methods.

Frequently asked questions

Which standard deviation is used?

Sample SD with n-1 denominator.

How are quartiles calculated?

Type-7 inclusive linear interpolation.

Are outliers removed?

No; 1.5 IQR flags are informational.

How is the confidence interval calculated?

A two-sided t-based 95 percent interval around the mean.

Is data uploaded?

No; processing stays in the browser.
