  3. Review units, method, intermediate values and flags before using an output.

About and limitations

The toolkit runs locally and supports planning and exploratory analysis, not diagnostic decisions. Dilution cannot convert between mass and molar concentration without molecular weight. qPCR assumes correctly identified groups and genes; flags are prompts for review, not automatic exclusions. GC% counts G and C over all IUPAC positions. Primer Tm does not test specificity, dimers or secondary structure and remains experimental. See methods and formula provenance.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Plant Lab Toolkit upload my data?

No. Calculations, pasted CSV data and sequence processing stay in this browser tab.

Which qPCR methods are available?

The qPCR tab supports Livak 2−ΔΔCt and efficiency-corrected Pfaffl calculations.

Is primer Tm validated for experimental use?

No. Primer Tm remains experimental and requires domain review; it does not assess specificity, dimers or secondary structure.
