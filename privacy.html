Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers a personal social media scheduling tool operated by Shoaib Munir for his own accounts only (X and LinkedIn). It is not a public service, and no one else can sign up or connect an account.

What the tool does

With the account owner's permission, it publishes posts that he has approved to his own X and LinkedIn profiles, at the times he chooses. It does not read other people's data, message anyone, or post on behalf of anyone else.

Data accessed and stored

  • Authorisation tokens for the owner's own LinkedIn and X accounts, stored encrypted on the owner's own computer.
  • The text and images of posts the owner writes, and their publishing status.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. Data stays on the owner's computer, apart from the posts themselves, which are sent to LinkedIn and X.

Website visitors

This website has no forms, cookies, analytics or tracking. The host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in LinkedIn and X account settings, and delete all stored data by removing the tool from his computer.
